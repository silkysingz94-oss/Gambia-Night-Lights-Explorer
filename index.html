14 / WORKFLOW

1. Load GBoS EA polygons with population and HHS attributes. The population choropleth is rendered directly on EA geography.

2. Zoom to a manageable geographic extent and retrieve OSM road ways.

3. Calculate each EA polygon centroid and its nearest loaded road distance.

4. Combine distance bands with mapped road hierarchy to assign a screening priority.

5. Summarise the full EA population attribute by screening class.

6. Export EA-level results for review and further GIS analysis.

15 / INTERPRETATION & LIMITATIONS

Not an acoustic noise model. This application does not calculate Lden, Lnight, Leq, sound pressure or dB(A). It does not include traffic volume, vehicle mix, speed, road surface, terrain, building geometry, façade orientation, meteorology or sound propagation.

Population and HHS figures are EA-level totals from the selected uploaded GBoS fields (map_pop / ls_pop / enum_pop / sum_pop and map_hhs / ls_hhs / enum_hhs / sum_hhs). The population choropleth maps the selected population attribute on EA geography. Screening summaries sum complete EA population and household totals for screened EAs; they do not identify residents or households physically located within a road buffer. These attributes are not individual household GPS point records. Results depend on the completeness and classification of OpenStreetMap roads and the date/version of the EA data.

For regulatory or public-health reporting, obtain traffic counts and measured sound-level data, then use a validated acoustic propagation method and field calibration.
